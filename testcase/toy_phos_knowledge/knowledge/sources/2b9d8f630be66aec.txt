Receptor module phosphorylation kinetics — a note (fixture document, 2024).

In cells expressing the receptor module, ligand addition drives phosphorylation of R to Rp
within minutes; a constitutive phosphatase returns Rp to R, and the steady-state fraction
phosphorylated rises with dose and saturates. Total receptor stays near 10 normalised units
over the first hour. Nothing here names a rate constant value.
